# Multi-division quotations

Selecting a division on a quotation switches:

1. **Catalog picker** — only that division’s products/services  
2. **PDF / proposal layout** — title, columns, sections, partner notes  

| Code | Brand | Format |
|------|--------|--------|
| 01 | RR Threads | Uniform inventory quotation (SKU, sizes, MOQ) |
| 02 | RR Wanders | Travel proposal & quotation |
| 03 | RR Marketing | Marketing proposal (retainers/projects — editable per client) |
| 04 | RR Connect | VA hours quotation; fulfilment via **Konekt+ (Manila)**; RR Connect invoices the client |
| 05 | RR Care | Care / medical travel quotation |
| 06 | RR Trading | Sourcing / trading quotation (“get what you want”) |

All scope notes, titles and closings are editable in **Settings → Division quotation formats**. Line prices stay editable per customer.

Shared company contacts remain on `/crm`. Division-specific ops (e.g. `/wanders`) stay separate.
