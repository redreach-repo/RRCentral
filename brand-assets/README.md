# Red Reach brand assets

Seed assets for the Red Reach tenant brand kit (spec section 5.1 and Appendix C).

- redreach/: the company logo (Red Reach Middle East FZE)
- redreach/divisions/: Connect, Marketing, Threads, Wanders (PNG with transparency, JPG on white)
- redreach/mark/: square R mark (8000 px source) and derived icon sizes
- clients/: client brands shown on account pages only (Tee Tribe)
- brand.json: machine readable manifest

Known limits: horizontal logos are only about 350 px wide, so they look soft in PDFs and on high density screens.
The division names under the logo are black, so they disappear on dark backgrounds.
There are no SVG sources yet, and no logos for RR Boost, RR Care or RR Trading.
