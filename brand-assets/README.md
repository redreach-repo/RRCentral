# Brand assets

## HighMast (product brand)

Seed assets for the HighMast product brand (Master Spec §5.2 and Appendix C.1).

- `highmast/highmast-symbol.png` — H with rising mast and orange light (route A)
- `highmast/highmast-stacked.png` — symbol above the HighMast wordmark
- `highmast/derived/` — square marks at 512 / 192 / 180 / 64 / 32 / 16 px (transparent canvas)
- `highmast/brand.json` — colours, file list, known gaps

Source PNGs ship with an opaque black background. SVG masters and light/dark colourways are still needed.

## Red Reach (tenant one)

Seed assets for the Red Reach tenant brand kit (spec §5.1 and Appendix C.2).

- `redreach/` — company logo (Red Reach Middle East FZE)
- `redreach/divisions/` — Connect, Marketing, Threads, Wanders (PNG with transparency, JPG on white)
- `redreach/mark/` — square R mark (8000 px source) and derived icon sizes
- `clients/` — client brands shown on account pages only (Tee Tribe)
- `brand.json` — machine readable Red Reach manifest

Known limits: horizontal logos are only about 350 px wide, so they look soft in PDFs and on high density screens.
The division names under the logo are black, so they disappear on dark backgrounds.
There are no SVG sources yet, and no logos for RR Boost, RR Care or RR Trading.
